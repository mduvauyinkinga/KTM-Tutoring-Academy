Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MathWithMDU processes personal information when you use our website. It is written for a South African audience and addresses the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. What personal information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, grade, plan status, and paid-through date.
  • Authentication data: processed by Firebase Authentication when you sign in.
  • Payments: email address for Paystack payment handling and a payment reference.
  • Content access: plan status used to determine which lessons and past papers you can access.
  • Admin uploads: file title, URL, uploader email, size, and upload time.
  • Support communications: messages and contact details you provide when contacting us.

2. Why we process personal information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide learning content based on your plan.
  • To process subscription payments via Paystack.
  • To allow administrators to upload and manage content.
  • To respond to subscription and support requests.

3. Consent and service delivery

Where consent is required, including consent recorded during registration, we will ask you to agree. Processing needed to provide account access and the service may also be necessary to perform or conclude a contract with you.

4. Cookies and tracking

The website may use cookies for essential functions and, where enabled, analytics or tracking. When non-essential cookies or analytics are used, we provide a cookie consent banner. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Who we share personal information with

  • Firebase / Google: authentication and Firestore database storage.
  • Paystack: payment processing.
  • WhatsApp / email providers: when you use those channels to contact us.

6. International data transfers

Firebase and Paystack are global services. Depending on their infrastructure and configuration, your information may be processed outside South Africa.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. No system is completely secure.

8. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide the service, manage subscriptions, and meet legal requirements. When it is no longer needed, we take steps to remove or de-identify it.

9. Your rights and privacy requests

You may ask us to:

  • Provide access to your personal information.
  • Correct or delete your personal information.
  • Consider an objection to processing where applicable.
  • Record withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to the relevant South African regulator.
