This page summarizes how to find information about MathWithMDU's handling of personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It is not a replacement for the Privacy Policy, which describes our processing in more detail.

Information and use

We process information such as account details, authentication data, plan status, payment references, upload metadata, and messages you send to us. We use it to manage accounts, provide learning content, process payments, and respond to support requests.

Information may be shared with the service providers used to operate these features, including Firebase/Google and Paystack. For more detail, see the Privacy Policy.
